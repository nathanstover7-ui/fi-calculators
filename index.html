Understand this illustration

This comparison follows the cash-conversion workbook method: the full loan amount stays invested, loan payments come from other funds, and investment growth is credited at a constant monthly amount within each year, with compounding at each 12-month anniversary. A partial year accrues proportionately. Loan interest is calculated monthly on the declining principal balance.

Growth minus loan interest is a limited illustration, not a complete wealth comparison. The cash buyer could invest the monthly payments avoided; that alternative is not modeled. Both scenarios assume the same boat, with no down payment, fees, taxes, investment costs, or tax deductions included.

Returns can vary and investments can lose value. The assumed return is your input, not a forecast. Estimates are not financial, investment, tax, or lending advice.

How the comparison rate is calculated

Extra payments reduce principal sooner, so less interest accrues. We calculate the total interest with the selected extra payment, then find the potential rate that would produce the same interest over the original term and financed amount.

This is a comparison tool, not a new loan offer, quoted APR, savings guarantee, or change to the contract rate. The estimate assumes a fixed rate, monthly payments, and all extra amounts applied to principal. It excludes taxes, insurance, fees, and any prepayment restrictions. Check the loan agreement and lender’s payment rules.

Products & prices

Drag a product into the order it should appear, or use the arrow buttons. Check a tier to include the product, then set the plan, term or miles, deductible, and this deal’s price. A gold field is a change from the remembered default.

Prices start blank. A checked tier still lists the product; its price counts as $0 until you enter one. Reset restores that product’s description, inclusion, and prices. Payments add the tier’s prices to the balance.

How this menu is calculated

Balance = sale price − trade allowance − cash & rebates + tax & fees. Each term’s payment without protection uses the standard payment formula on that balance at that term’s rate (balance ÷ number of payments when the rate is 0%).

A tier’s price is the sum of the prices entered for products checked in that tier. A blank price counts as $0. Each checkbox is that same formula on balance + tier price, at that column’s rate and term. Products are listed in the priority order set above. Reset puts a product back to the description, inclusion, and prices you remembered or last loaded.

Plan names, terms, deductibles, and prices come from the provider’s current rate card and contract. Buying a protection product is not required to obtain financing. Payments are estimates.
